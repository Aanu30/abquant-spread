## Reading

- The fix was picked as the best of five on y1-y48, so its +365 there flatters it. On the 86 weeks that were not used to pick it the difference is +26 a week (se 83): no proven gain.
- The t, v, s and w weeks were used to tune the bot that played, so they lean against any change. The x weeks are fresh for both versions and are the fairest single read: +136 a week (se 112).
- All 134 weeks together: +147 a week (se 70). So the fair statement is a gain somewhere between nothing and about 150 a week, with 23 fewer cars a week going alone, the same cars won and the same share on time on average, and three more weeks between 90% and 92% on time.
- Drivers do take three-car gigs at once (0.76 x the typical fees, within 2 minutes, 1% of their cars late). Driver fees fall by 265 a week; 62 of that goes back in late refunds and 61 in lower revenue (one car fewer won a week).

## What our stats would have been

The secret weeks of the final and of the ranking rounds cannot be played again, so this adds the backtest's difference to our real scores and reads the place off the real tables. Three readings of the difference: +26 a week (the 86 weeks not used to pick the fix), +136 (the fresh x weeks), +147 (all 134 weeks). Cars won and the share on time do not change in the backtest.

So the best of the fixes would have left us about where we were: 23rd to 25th in the final and just outside the top ten in the ranking rounds. The top ten of the final needed 1,954 more in that one week; the top ten of the ranking rounds needed 154 to 182 more a week.
